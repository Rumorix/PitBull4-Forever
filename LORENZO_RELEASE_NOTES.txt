- Party/Raid Pet range: use owner range as an approximation for pet range and forward potentially secret booleans to the existing Blizzard-safe alpha setter. (Inherited from v58 RangePetToTAura.)
- Target of Target: retain the explicit unit-target aura refresh, plus two experimental combat-only delayed refreshes at 0.12 and 0.40 seconds. These are not confirmed fixes in a live game client. (Inherited from v58/v59.)
- Health: preserve a linear health color curve instead of step transitions; health-percent interpolation still uses the Blizzard curve path. (Inherited from v60.)
